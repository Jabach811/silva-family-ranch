Where to find us

Tracy, California.

Hay pickup is in Tracy, CA 95304, by appointment only. Call Mike first — there's a 15 bale minimum and we need to know you're coming.

Beef is collected from the butcher shop, not the ranch. We'll send you the address and the pickup window once your harvest date is set.

Our cattle graze hillsides in Alameda and Contra Costa Counties and irrigated pastures in San Joaquin County. It's a working ranch, so we don't have drop-in hours.
