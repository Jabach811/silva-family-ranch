About Michael

He'd rather grow the feed than milk the cows.

Michael grew up on his father's dairy farm here in Tracy.

He liked caring for the cattle well enough. But what he really loved was growing the feed that fed them.

He started custom hay work for neighboring farms while he was still in high school, and he's still doing it today, now with his family alongside him.

Michael, Rosie and their three children put real pride into the alfalfa, orchard grass, oats and wheat that come off this ground.
