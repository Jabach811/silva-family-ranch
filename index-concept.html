From Rosie

A ranch built one heifer at a time.

My parents gave me a Holstein heifer when I graduated. I raised her, sold her, and used the money to buy three more. I kept building from there.

Today Michael, our three children, and I raise beef and grow hay across the Central Valley — cattle on hillsides and irrigated pastures, hay from ground we know by heart.

Now I'm raising beef with my own family and selling it directly to families like yours. What more could I ask for?
