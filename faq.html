Beef and hay questions

Do you sell beef by the pound?

Not from the ranch. Selling whole, half and quarter shares is what keeps our overhead low, and that saving goes to you.

If you want beef by the cut, two local shops carry it: The Urban Edge Farm and Moondust Farms Market.

What's the difference between grass finished and grain finished?

All our cattle are raised on native grasses, here in the Central Valley and in the coastal range hills. Several months before harvest, we either keep them grazing on grass only, or move them onto a high-quality mixed grain ration.

Grass-finished is leaner with a stronger flavor. Grain-finished has more fat and a milder, sweeter taste. It comes down to what you prefer. Our most popular order is a half of grass-finished beef.

If you're debating, get in touch and we'll talk it through.

What does “finished” mean?

It's a farming term for how an animal is fed in the final weeks before harvest. That's all it means.

What is dry aging?

The cleaned carcass hangs in a temperature-controlled cooler at the butcher shop for 14 to 28 days before it's cut and wrapped.

Two things happen while it hangs. Moisture evaporates from the muscle, which concentrates the beef flavor. And the beef's own enzymes break down connective tissue, which makes it more tender.

It's the same process behind the beef you'd pay a premium for at a high-end steakhouse.

How much freezer space will I need?

One cubic foot of freezer space holds roughly 35 to 40 pounds of vacuum-sealed beef.

An average quarter order of about 100 pounds of grass-finished beef needs around 3 cubic feet. Scale up from there for a half or a whole.

How is the price worked out?

Cost is based on hanging weight — what the carcass weighs after the hide, head and internal organs are removed. That's usually 55 to 62 percent of the animal's live weight.

Pricing moves with the market, so we don't publish a fixed number. Email us for the current information sheet and we'll send you pricing and the standard cut list.

Do I need to find someone to split a half or quarter with?

No. We match up share orders ourselves so they add up to a whole animal. You just tell us what size you want.

Do you ship?

Not at this time. We do have out-of-area customers who pick up their beef while visiting. Plenty of people have driven a long way home with Silva Ranch beef packed in coolers with dry ice.

How do I order?

Send us an order request. Once we confirm availability we'll ask for a deposit to reserve your share.
